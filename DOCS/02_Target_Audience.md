## 🎯 Primary Target Segments

### 1. Tourists & Backpackers

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Demographics** | Age 18-45, domestic & international travelers |
| **Pain Point** | Need to explore cities but carrying heavy bags |
| **Behavior** | Booking accommodation via MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Hostelworld |
| **Usage** | Store bags while sightseeing, temple visits, shopping |
| **Revenue Potential** | High — Multiple hours of storage, premium for NFC tokens |

**Kutch-Specific:** Rann Utsav visitors (November-February), Dholavira tourists, Bhujodi handicraft shoppers

---

### 2. Train & Bus Travelers

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Demographics** | Age 20-55, frequent interstate travelers |
| **Pain Point** | Arrive early, departure is hours later; no secure storage |
| **Behavior** | Use IRCTC, RedBus, State Transport |
| **Usage** | Short-term storage (3-8 hours) near stations |
| **Revenue Potential** | High volume — large numbers passing through daily |

**Gujarat-Specific Stations:**
- Bhuj Railway Station
- Gandhidham Junction
- Ahmedabad Junction
- Surat Railway Station
- Vadodara Junction

---

### 3. Students & Daily Commuters

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Demographics** | Age 17-25, college students traveling between cities |
| **Pain Point** | Heavy bags during inter-city travel for exams, interviews, visits |
| **Behavior** | Budget-conscious, prefer UPI payments |
| **Usage** | 6-12 hours storage during exams, internship interviews |
| **Revenue Potential** | Medium — Price-sensitive but high volume |

**Gujarat Universities:** Gujarat University, CEPT, IIT Gandhinagar, Nirma University

---

### 4. Business Travelers

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Demographics** | Age 28-50, professionals, SME owners |
| **Pain Point** | Need to move light between meetings; don't want to carry laptop bags + luggage |
| **Behavior** | Use airports, Ola/Uber, coworking spaces |
| **Usage** | Premium quick-access storage, NFC tokens preferred |
| **Revenue Potential** | High — Willing to pay premium for convenience |

**Gujarat Business Hubs:** GIFT City, Ahmedabad, Surat Diamond Hub, Mundra Port Area

---

### 5. Forgotten Luggage Customers

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Demographics** | All ages, any traveler who left bags behind |
| **Pain Point** | Left bag at hotel/station, no easy retrieval |
| **Behavior** | Desperate, willing to pay for courier delivery |
| **Usage** | Delivery service (one-time, high-value transaction) |
| **Revenue Potential** | Very High — ₹300-1,500 per delivery arrangement |
