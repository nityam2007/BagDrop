## 🤝 Partner Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Partner Identification
- Identify shops near transit hubs (railway stations, bus stands)
- Target: Local general stores, cafes, hotels, pharmacies

### Step 2: Verification
- KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, GST)
- Physical verification of premises
- Background check (police verification if needed)

### Step 3: Agreement
- Sign BagDrop Partner Agreement
- Terms: Revenue share (70:30 — Partner:BagDrop)
- Insurance and liability clauses

### Step 4: Training
- Provide training kit (video + manual in Gujarati/Hindi)
- App walkthrough for partner dashboard
- Security protocols and emergency handling

### Step 5: Go Live
- Activate location on BagDrop app
- Monitor initial transactions
- Weekly quality check for first month

## ⚖️ Partner Agreement Terms

| Clause | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Revenue Share** | 70% Partner, 30% BagDrop |
| **Payment Cycle** | Weekly settlement via bank transfer |
| **Liability** | Partner liable for loss up to ₹5,000; BagDrop insurance covers above |
| **Termination** | 15 days written notice by either party |
| **Minimum Hours** | Partner must provide 10 AM - 8 PM access |
