##  Vision Statement

> **"To become India's leading smart luggage storage and delivery network, empowering every traveler to explore freely without the burden of their bags."**

We envision a future where no traveler in India — whether a tourist, student, or business professional — has to drag heavy luggage through crowded stations, miss out on exploring new places, or worry about forgotten bags. BagDrop will be synonymous with hassle-free travel across the nation.

##  Mission Statement

> **"To provide secure, contactless, and affordable luggage storage solutions through a network of verified local partners, enabling travelers to store their bags anywhere and pick them up anytime — with the added assurance of nationwide delivery for forgotten luggage."**

##  The Problem We Solve

> *"Imagine you've checked out of your hotel at 10 a.m., but your train is at 7 p.m. You want to explore the city… but your luggage says no. You drag it through cafes, metros, crowded streets — and suddenly, travel feels like a chore, not a vibe."*

### Key Pain Points:
1. **Early Checkout, Late Departure** — Hotels force checkout but flights/trains are hours away
2. **Heavy Bags, Ruined Plans** — Dragging luggage kills the travel experience
3. **Overpriced Cloakrooms** — Railway lockers cost ₹100+/day, often full or broken
4. **Forgotten Luggage** — No easy way to retrieve bags left behind

**Statistic:** 70% of travelers face luggage problems during their trip.

---

##  Our Solution

**BagDrop** — A smart, affordable, contactless luggage storage and delivery network, built for travelers who want freedom.

### How It Works:
1. **FIND** — Open app, locate nearby BagDrop location
2. **BOOK** — Select slot, pay via UPI/Card/NFC
3. **DROP** — Leave bags, get OTP/NFC token
4. **RETRIEVE** — Show token, collect bags
5. **DELIVER** — Forgot bag? We courier it anywhere in India

**Average drop time: Under 2 minutes**

- Provide training kit (video + manual in Gujarati/Hindi)

- Monitor initial transactions
- Weekly quality check for first month
