## 🛡️ Exit Protection Mechanisms

### For Founders

| Mechanism | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Drag-Along Rights** | Founders can force minority to sell in acquisition |
| **Tag-Along Rights** | Can join if majority sells |
| **ESOP Pool** | 10% for team, vested over 4 years |
| **Non-compete** | 2-year post-exit restriction |

### For Investors

| Mechanism | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Liquidation Preference** | 1x preferred return before founders |
| **Anti-dilution** | Protection against down-rounds |
| **Board Seat** | Governance representation |

## ⚠️ Exit Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Low revenue growth | Lower valuation | Aggressive marketing, pricing optimization |
| Competition entry | Market share loss | USP focus (delivery), partner lock-in |
| Tech obsolescence | Reduced IP value | Continuous R&D, IoT upgrades |
| Regulatory changes | Compliance costs | Legal counsel, industry body membership |
| Economic downturn | Delayed exit | Maintain runway, profitable operations |
| Key person risk | Loss of leadership | ESOP, succession planning |

## 🎯 Exit Criteria

### Minimum Thresholds for Exit

| Metric | Minimum | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Valuation | ₹25 Crore | ₹50+ Crore |
| Investor Multiple | 5x | 10x+ |
| Founder Retain Post-Exit | 1 year | 2 years |
| Employee Protection | 6-month guarantee | ESOP acceleration |

### Deal Breakers

- Valuation below ₹20 Crore
- Investor return below 3x
- Forced layoffs without severance
- Non-compete beyond 3 years
- Loss of brand identity without premium
