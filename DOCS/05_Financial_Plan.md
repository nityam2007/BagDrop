|---------|----------|--------------|
| Storage Services | 18% | SAC 996714 |
| Delivery/Courier | 18% (if applicable) | SAC 996812 |
| Technology Platform Fee | 18% | SAC 998314 |

### Tax Planning

| Tax | Rate | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| Corporate Tax (Startup) | 15% | Under Section 115BAB (new manufacturing) or 25% regular |
| TDS on Partner Payments | 2% | Section 194C for contractor payments |
| GST Input Credit | Claimable | On tech, marketing, office expenses |

### Startup India Benefits
- **Tax Holiday:** 3 years out of first 10 years (80-IAC)
- **Angel Tax Exemption:** DPIIT registered startups
- **Self-Certification:** Labour & environment laws

## 🔐 Financial Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low bookings | Aggressive referral program, dynamic pricing |
| Partner defaults | Weekly settlements, security deposit (₹5,000) |
| Fraud/Theft | Insurance coverage, partner verification, CCTV requirement |
| Payment disputes | Clear T&C, escrow for high-value items |
| Competition | Focus on delivery feature (USP), local partnerships |
