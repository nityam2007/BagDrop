**Common Questions to Prepare:**

### 1. "How do you ensure bags are safe?"
**Answer (Member 1 or 3):**
> "Three layers of security: First, all partners are verified with CCTV. Second, every bag is insured up to Rs.25,000. Third, OTP/NFC ensures only the rightful owner retrieves the bag. Plus, 48-hour claim resolution."

### 2. "What's your competitive advantage?"
**Answer (Member 2):**
> "Three things: One, we're cheaper – Rs.50 vs Rs.100+ for railway lockers. Two, our forgotten luggage delivery is unique – no competitor offers this. Three, we're app-first with UPI payments, while competitors are cash-only and offline."

### 3. "How do you onboard partner shops?"
**Answer (Member 3):**
> "Simple: 70% revenue share for the shop, no lock-in, weekly payouts. We verify the location, train the staff with a simple checklist, and provide marketing materials. Partners earn passive income from unused space."

### 4. "What's your initial investment requirement?"
**Answer (Member 3):**
> "Rs.36 lakh seed funding. Rs.5 lakh for tech, Rs.18 lakh for 6-month runway, Rs.6.5 lakh for marketing, Rs.3 lakh for equipment, Rs.3.25 lakh contingency. This gets us to profitability."

### 5. "How do you handle lost or damaged luggage?"
**Answer (Member 1 or 3):**
> "Every bag is insured up to Rs.25,000. Claims are processed instantly with 48-hour resolution. Partners with repeated issues are de-listed. We take full responsibility."

### 6. "Why Gujarat? Why not start in a metro city?"
**Answer (Member 1):**
> "Strategic first-mover advantage. Rann Utsav brings 5 lakh visitors in peak season – captive audience. Lower operational costs than metros. Once proven here, we scale to major cities with stronger unit economics."

### 7. "How will you compete if a well-funded player enters?"
**Answer (Member 2):**
> "First-mover advantage is powerful in partner networks. By the time a competitor enters, we'll have exclusive partnerships and local brand trust. Our delivery USP is also hard to replicate – it requires logistics infrastructure."

### 8. "What's your customer acquisition cost?"
**Answer (Member 3):**
> "Target CAC of Rs.20-30 per user. With Rs.50 average booking and 30% margin, we recover CAC in 2-3 bookings. Referral program and organic word-of-mouth will lower CAC over time."
